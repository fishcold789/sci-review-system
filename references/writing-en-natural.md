# Natural academic English

Use a two-pass route: faithful translation first, naturalization second. Keep
the English draft aligned with the scientific baseline before adapting it to a
journal profile.

## Faithful pass

Preserve claims, scope, modal verbs, negation, attribution, numbers, units,
conditions, citations, figures, equations, and section roles. Adjust grammar,
articles, tense, and word order for correct English, but do not add claims.

For LaTeX, protect commands, math, labels, citations, cross-references and
escaped special characters. For Markdown/Word, protect tables, links, footnotes
and code-like spans.

## Naturalization pass

Prefer common concrete verbs and direct subjects. Reduce unnecessary
nominalization, stacked passive clauses, generic transitions, dense parenthesis,
excessive em dashes, and inflated words such as `game-changing` or
`comprehensive` when no measurable basis exists. Do not turn correlation into
causation or `suggests/may` into `proves/demonstrates`.

Do not force prose into lists or shorten a paragraph into an abstract. Journal
profiles may change format and diction, never the scientific meaning.

## Required output

Return the faithful draft, the naturalized draft, a modification ledger, a
protected-span report, and the bilingual back-check input. If no change is
needed, record `NO_CHANGE_NEEDED` rather than inventing edits.
