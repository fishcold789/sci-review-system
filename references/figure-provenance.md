# Figure, citation, and rights provenance

Bind every figure or table to its function, first citation, source paper/page,
original figure or panel, crop/recomposition, hash, license state, and final
path. A figure without a traceable source is not a reusable asset.

```text
claim -> figure/table -> source/page/panel -> crop/hash -> rights state -> first citation
```

Keep scientific figure usefulness, citation mapping, and legal permission as
separate checks. Do not call a screenshot or adapted panel original. Tables use
the configured journal style; when a three-line table is required, keep the
reference source in the final column or caption policy.
