# Terminology, formula, and stance protection

Every project may maintain a terminology ledger with states:

`preferred`, `acceptable`, `ambiguous`, `forbidden`, `needs_human_confirmation`.

Protect technical terms, abbreviations, formulas, symbols, numbers, units,
conditions, citation positions, and stance markers. Record changes as
`terminology_fix`, not as untraceable rewriting. Do not upgrade “可能、提示、
表明、支持” to “证明、必然、建立了因果关系”.

When a term, formula, algorithm name, or experimental setting is ambiguous,
create an uncertainty record and human checkpoint instead of selecting a
plausible synonym silently.
