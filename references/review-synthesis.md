# Cross-literature review synthesis

Write a review as an analysis of relationships, not a sequence of paper
summaries. For every synthesis block, make the comparison dimension explicit.

## Synthesis questions

- What problem does this method or concept solve?
- What new assumption, calibration burden, computation, data requirement, or
  failure mode does it introduce?
- Why do studies disagree: population or study object, intervention/method,
  input data, measurement conditions, metric definition, reference standard,
  dataset, protocol, confounders, bias, or implementation?
- Which limitation is technical, which is data-related, and which is a
  validation/reporting limitation?
- What evidence supports the proposed gap, and what testable question follows?

Do not equate chronology with progress, list papers without a shared dimension,
or turn a convenient absence of evidence into a research gap. A synthesis must
record supporting and contradicting sources and its confidence state.

## Comparison block

```yaml
comparison_id: CMP-001
dimension: "input / mechanism / calibration / output / validation"
methods: []
common_context: "..."
differences: []
supporting_evidence: []
contradicting_evidence: []
limits: []
confidence: verified | supported | uncertain | human_review_required
next_question: "..."
```
