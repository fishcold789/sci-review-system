# Editorial correspondence templates (English)

> Keep only the block that matches the current situation and adapt it to the actual
> correspondence, manuscript state, and journal instructions. Every `{{...}}` field
> requires author verification. AI may draft; the corresponding author verifies the
> recipient, facts, attachments, and wording and sends the message. Do not present a
> formal response without a real decision letter. Do not say that a change was made
> unless it exists in the revised manuscript.
