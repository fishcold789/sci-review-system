Amplitude-based sizing rests on a comparison against a reference reflector, and the calibration block must match the component in material, surface finish and attenuation (which depends on manufacturing and heat treatment; and on the coupling condition; and on the probe size). Krautkramer et al. [12] originated this approach, and the resulting curve is now embedded in standards. The method is comprehensive.
